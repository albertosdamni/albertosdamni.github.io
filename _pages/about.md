I am a lecturer in Islamic Religious Education (PAI) and a Doctoral Candidate in Educational Research and Evaluation, with a specialized concentration in Educational Measurement and Advanced Psychometrics. 

My academic journey is dedicated to bridging multicultural education with rigorous educational measurement. I am passionate about applying advanced quantitative methodologies to understand complex educational, psychological, and socio-religious phenomena in Indonesia.

### Research Overview
My work primarily utilizes advanced statistical frameworks to develop, validate, and refine diagnostic instruments and psychological scales. Currently, my research portfolio encompasses three main thematic areas:

* **Educational Measurement and Psychometric Validation:** Constructing robust measurement instruments to assess latent traits accurately, utilizing tools like Item Response Theory (IRT) and Rasch Modeling to ensure high standards of reliability and construct validity.
* **The Intersection of Education, Science, and Religion:** Exploring the sociological and psychological dynamics where scientific education and religious beliefs converge, such as how prospective teachers conceptualize professionalism and how religious paradigms influence societal perspectives.
* **Socio-Behavioral Dynamics in Learning and Society:** Analyzing student satisfaction with learning methodologies in the digital era, deciphering motives driving academic choices, and evaluating community responses to shifting social norms within religious contexts.

Through my research and teaching endeavors, I aim to cultivate spaces where students and fellow educators can engage meaningfully with multicultural perspectives, ultimately contributing to more inclusive, adaptive, and evidence-based educational policies.
