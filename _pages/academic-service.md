I am actively involved in the academic community and am open to invitations to serve as a peer reviewer for international journals and conferences. 

My methodological expertise allows me to provide rigorous, constructive, and detailed evaluations, particularly for manuscripts that utilize quantitative methodologies and advanced psychometric analyses.

### Methodological Expertise for Review
I am highly proficient in evaluating studies that employ the following methodologies and frameworks:
* **Item Response Theory (IRT):** Unidimensional and multidimensional models.
* **Rasch Modeling:** Application in instrument development and validation.
* **Structural Equation Modeling (SEM):** Covariance-based SEM (CB-SEM) and Partial Least Squares (PLS-SEM).
* **Cognitive Diagnostic Models (CDM):** Assessment of specific cognitive skills and attributes.
* **Instrument Validation:** Classical Test Theory (CTT), reliability, and validity testing.
* **Quantitative Research Methods:** Experimental, quasi-experimental, and correlational designs in educational contexts.

### How to Request a Review
Journal editors or conference organizers who wish to invite me for a peer review assignment can contact me directly via my institutional email: **albertosdamni@iainkerinci.ac.id**. I am committed to providing timely and comprehensive feedback to help maintain the quality of academic publishing.
