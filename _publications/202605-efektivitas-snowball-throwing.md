Low student learning motivation in the Islamic Religious Education (PAI) subject-specifically regarding the topic of the Life of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH)-poses a challenge that can hinder student engagement and the achievement of learning objectives. This study aims to analyze the effectiveness of the Snowball Throwing method on the learning motivation of fourth-grade students at SDN 77/III Mukai Tinggi.
